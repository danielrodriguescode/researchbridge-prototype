Who is your audience?

Policy Maker

Government officials, health ministry staff, regulatory bodies. They need evidence to justify decisions — quickly.

🩺
Clinical Team

Doctors, nurses, allied health professionals. They need practical implications, not statistical methods.

👥
Patient Group

Patients, carers, and patient advocates. They need accessible language and focus on what it means for them.

🌍
General Public

Journalists, community groups, social media audiences. They need engaging, jargon-free summaries.
